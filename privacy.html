Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how the website at rogerloomis.us (the "Site"), the personal website of Roger Loomis, collects, uses, and protects your information. The Site is built and maintained on Roger's behalf by Adam Loomis Marketing. If you have any questions, you can reach us at contact@adamloomis.online.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and any message you send through our contact form
  • Your email address if you subscribe to receive updates
  • Basic, non-identifying technical data your browser sends automatically (such as device type and pages visited), used only to keep the Site working and to understand general usage

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your questions, comments, and prayer or speaking requests
  • Send updates or encouragement if you have asked to receive them
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Site

4. Cookies

The Site uses a small amount of browser storage to remember your cookie preference so we do not show the cookie notice again after you have responded. We may also use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the Site is used in aggregate. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Messages submitted through the contact form are delivered to us by email through a third-party form delivery service. We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our records, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it from unauthorized access.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your information is shared only with the service providers that help us operate the Site (such as our website host and form delivery service), and only to the extent needed to provide those services. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Third-Party Links

The Site links to third-party platforms, including Amazon (for Roger's books) and YouTube. When you follow those links, the privacy practices of those companies apply. We encourage you to review their policies, as we are not responsible for their content or practices.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at contact@adamloomis.online and we will respond promptly.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
