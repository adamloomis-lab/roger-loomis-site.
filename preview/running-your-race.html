Running Your Race

A Guide for New Christ Followers

← All Books
Let us run with perseverance the race marked out for us.Hebrews 12:1
From the Foreword

Running Your Race was written to help new believers effectively run their Christian race. I have a burden to place this tool into the hands of those who come to faith in Christ.

This material is divided into eight sections, and is designed for a one-on-one approach to discipleship. It works best when new believers are assigned one accountability partner. So, on your mark, get ready, set… GO!

From Section One, Leaving the Starting Block

Congratulations! You accepted Jesus Christ as your personal Savior. Now your goal is to make Him Lord! The Bible says, “There is rejoicing in the presence of the angels in heaven when just one sinner repents” (Luke 15:10). Heaven is by appointment only and you just made your reservation. How wonderful!

You were saved by faith. If you liken what happened to a race, you just left the starting block! You must now run your race with patience and perseverance.

Salvation is just the beginning. God’s continued plan is that you grow to maturity. Now that you have turned your life over to Christ, you will from this point on learn to think, act and talk like your new Master…
